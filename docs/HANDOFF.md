### 3.5 Recently Completed: Substep 4.2.2 (`Dual-Pass Illumination Flattening & Anti-Starvation`)
Implemented dual-pass background quotient flattening and multiplicative anti-starvation contrast leveling in [`Sources/ScanTailorCore/Binarization/BackgroundNormalizer.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorCore/Binarization/BackgroundNormalizer.swift), plus outlier-robust Wolf-Jolion anchors in [`AdaptiveBinarizer.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorCore/Binarization/AdaptiveBinarizer.swift):
1. **Configuration & Telemetry** (backward-compatible `decodeIfPresent`): `enableDualPassFlattening` (`true`), `secondPassBlurSigmaScale` (`0.65`), `enableAntiStarvationContrastLeveling` (`true`), `maximumContrastGain` (`2.50`, `[1.0, 3.5]`); `DerivedKernelParameters` gains `dualPassApplied`, `secondPassDownsampledBlurSigma`, `contrastLevelingApplied`, `peakContrastGain`.
2. **Pass 1 → Pass 2 residual background**: unclipped `UInt16` quotient $Q_1 = 255 \cdot I / B_1$; a **mode-seeking per-block crest** (two successive trimmed upper means, `q1 >= 15/16 * current`) closed by slope-aware `closeSlopeAwareUInt16`; pure-paper sampling at $Q_1 \ge 0.88 \cdot \text{crest}$ with **PSF-skirt rejection** (candidates below $0.96 \cdot \text{crest}$ that touch a foreground pixel, $Q_1 \le 0.78 \cdot \text{crest}$, are discarded); multi-stride diffusion inpainting; and normalized Gaussian smoothing with **point-reflected boundaries** (`SmoothingBoundary.linearExtrapolate`, $v(-k) = 2v(0) - v(k)$), which reproduces linear edge ramps exactly. $B_{\text{eff}} = B_1 \cdot B_2 / 255$ in Q6 fixed point.
3. **Anti-starvation contrast leveling**: the ink-core absorption envelope is sampled only from paper-supported cells, rejecting outliers above `p90 + 40` (desk strips / black borders). The crisp reference is `clamp(p90, 145, 175)` and the gain is $g = \text{target}/\text{env}$, applied **multiplicatively** ($A' = A \cdot (1 + (g-1)\,w)$, because veiling glare compresses absorption multiplicatively). The smoothstep gate $w$ (absorption 16 → 40) is driven by the **3×3 peak absorption**, so stroke-edge pixels inherit their stroke's gain (uniform stroke weight) while isolated grain and bleed-through (absorption ≤ 16) stay untouched.
4. **Root causes fixed while closing the last 3 tests**:
   - *Spine gutter cloud (1476 → 0 false positives at `x = 4..6`)*: the Pass 2 clamp-to-edge Gaussian over-estimated the steep spine ramp in the outermost cells, so normalized paper there was only 185–215. The point-reflected boundary fixes this.
   - *Stroke-weight non-uniformity (Wolf-Jolion 7 px upper vs 9 px lower → 9/9; Sauvola 7/7)*: three compounding causes. (a) The affine boost `16 + g·(A − 16)` under-boosted 25 %-coverage PSF skirts. (b) The x < 22 desk strip read as "ink" at absorption ≈ 238 and inflated the envelope. (c) Faint veiled skirts (0.92 × paper) passed the 0.88 paper gate and biased $B_{\text{eff}}$ 1.9 % low in veiled text rows (now +0.05 %), which the gain then amplified.
   - *Dense columns*: the old fixture left clean 4×4 blocks above short glyphs (Pass 1 MAE 0.14, so there was nothing to recover). The fixture now sets uniform 24-px bodies solid through a realistic 3×3 binomial PSF (85 % ink absorption): MAE₁ = 9.07, MAE₂ = 0.27. Under that realistic PSF the previous crest/paper gate made Pass 2 *worse* than Pass 1, which the mode-seeking crest and skirt rejection fix.
5. **Tests (`103/103` passing across 18 suites, 0 warnings on a clean build)**:
   - [`Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/DualPassIlluminationTests.swift`](../Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/DualPassIlluminationTests.swift) (5 tests). These cover Simpson glare/gradient stroke-weight uniformity (Wolf-Jolion & Sauvola), dense multi-column Pass 2 recovery, the steep spine shadow (plus a direct linear-reflection invariant check), the bleed-through/grain noise gate, and Codable/edge cases/`BookPipelineCoordinator` Pass 1 uniformity.

### 3.6 Recently Completed: Substep 5.1.1 (`DocumentQuadDetector` — Paper Edge & Contrast Boundary Detection)
A standalone 4-corner document quadrangle estimator in [`Sources/ScanTailorCore/Geometry/DocumentQuadDetector.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorCore/Geometry/DocumentQuadDetector.swift), with pure-Swift kernels in [`QuadDetectionKernels.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorCore/Geometry/QuadDetectionKernels.swift). It is wired into `BookPipelineCoordinator` and the manifest by Substep 5.1.2 (§3.7).
1. **Public API** (`Sendable`, `Equatable`, `Codable`, with `decodeIfPresent` defaults):
   - `DocumentQuad`: TL/TR/BR/BL corners in continuous source pixels, plus `corners`, `area`, `boundingBox`, `contains(_:)` and `fullFrame(width:height:)`.
   - `DocumentQuadDetection`: `outcome` (`detected` / `fullFrame` / `rejected`), `quad` (full frame unless detected), `candidateQuad`, `confidence`, per-side `sideSupport` and `sideResidualPixels` (`SideMetrics` struct), `boundaryContrast`, `areaFraction`, and `flags` (`occluded:<side>`, `bentSide:<side>`, `lowContrastDesk`, `cornerFallback:<corner>`, `noDeskDetected`, `rejected:<reason>`).
   - `DocumentQuadDetector(configuration:).detect(in:)` never throws.
2. **Algorithm**:
   - Integer area-average to a working raster with a long side of at most 1024 px.
   - Paper region: score `localMean − localStd`, Otsu split (with a second split of the bright class when dark text would otherwise group a light desk with the paper), closing, dominant components, hole fill, convex hull, then the maximum-area inscribed quad.
   - Multi-scale Canny (σ = 1/2/4, hysteresis thresholds from the median gradient) keeping edges that persist across scales, point dark-outside → bright-inside, and actually separate region from non-region. This rejects text and wood grain.
   - Per-side deterministic RANSAC (SplitMix64) + TLS. Sides whose end segments disagree by more than 1.5° are `bentSide` (open-book spine dips) and use corner-local segment lines.
   - Full-resolution sub-pixel refinement along perpendicular profiles with MAD-trimmed refits. Corners are line intersections, so occluded corners are extrapolated.
   - Validation: convexity, corner angles within 90° ± 35°, side proportions, area, side support, contrast, and a geometric-mean confidence. A frame-filling region counts as `fullFrame` only if the 1–3 % border ring is raw paper-class (≥ 80 %); otherwise it is rejected, which is how a wood-only image is rejected.
3. **Accuracy on synthetic fixtures** (1800×1350, 3×3 PSF, noise σ = 3): corner errors ≤ 0.07 px for the wood desk, low-contrast laminate (contrast 35, confidence 0.90), thumb occlusion of the left side and bottom-left corner, the bent open-book spread, and the 0.5× rescale.
4. **Debug-build performance**: blur, downsample and morphology use pointer loops (the Gaussian went from 4.0 s to 0.15 s per σ = 4 pass); the suite runs in about 16 s.
5. **Tests**: [`Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/DocumentQuadDetectorTests.swift`](../Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/DocumentQuadDetectorTests.swift) (8 tests) covering the wooden desk, low-contrast desk, partial occlusion, calibrated no-desk scan, wood-only and plain-grey rejection, bent open-book spread, scale invariance/determinism, and Codable/degenerate inputs. `111/111` passed across 19 suites at the time, 0 warnings on a clean build.

   - `estimateAspectRatio(of:imageSize:)`: Zhang–He whiteboard method (principal point = image center). The focal length is solved from the two vanishing directions when plausible (0.3–4 × diagonal); otherwise (parallelogram, one-axis trapezoid) an assumed `0.8 × diagonal` is used.
   - `plan(for: DocumentQuadDetection, …)` returns `nil` for `fullFrame`/`rejected` (passthrough). `plan(for: DocumentQuad, …)` refuses non-convex/anticlockwise quads. With a known `--page-size`, an aspect within 15 % of the single-page or two-page-spread aspect snaps to it (`aspectSource = .pageSize`).
   - Output size: no quad edge is downsampled; total pixels capped at 1.25 × source. Quad corners map exactly to the output corners (no inset; border shadows are left to Phase 5.2).
   - `rectify(_:plan:)` / `resample(_:inverse:outputSize:)`: pure-Swift bilinear, incremental per-row homography, pixel-center convention, out-of-source samples = 255, DPI preserved. No `ComputeAccelerator` requirement yet.
   - `RectificationPlan` (`Codable`): `quad`, `aspectRatio`, `aspectSource` (`perspective` / `assumedFocalLength` / `pageSize`), `focalLengthPixels`, `outputSize`, `sourceToRectified`, `rectifiedToSource`.
3. **Pipeline** ([`BookPipelineCoordinator.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorPipeline/BookPipelineCoordinator.swift), [`SourcePageGeometry.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorPipeline/SourcePageGeometry.swift)): step 0 of the Pass-1 loop, after font-scale resampling and before `PageSplitEstimator`. The font-scale probe pass still sees unrectified pages.
4. **Manifest & overrides**: `BookProcessingManifest.sourcePageGeometry: [SourcePageGeometryRecord]` (`decodeIfPresent`, so older manifests load) with `sourcePageIndex`, `rasterSize`, `detection?`, `rectified`, `plan?`, `manualOverride?`. `SourcePageGeometryOverride` (keyed by **source** page, because rectification precedes the split) has `overrideQuad` (bypasses detection, applies even when automatic rectification is off, rescaled by the `rasterSize` ratio when the raster changes) and `disableRectification`. Overrides are applied through the existing `--manifest` re-run path and carried forward.
5. **CLI**: `--no-perspective` (alias `--no-quad`); the manifest summary prints `Perspective Rectification: N rectified | N full-frame | N rejected | N manual override(s)`.
6. **Accuracy & performance** (keystone fixture: 1400×1050, pitch 24°, yaw −15°, roll 4°, wood desk): the detected plan maps the true 5×5 page grid within 1 px, aspect error 0.03 %, text lines straight (RMS ≤ 0.5 px). Exact pinhole projections recover aspect and focal length to 1e-6. Debug build: detection ≈ 0.5 s on a 5100×6600 page; rectification ≈ 0.05 s for 586×879.

### 3.8 Recently Completed: Substep 5.2.1 (Pre-Binarization Table Surface Masking)
Release Criterion 1 is now met for residual desk after rectification and for pages where rectification found no usable quad: both are filled with a paper-toned estimate before `BackgroundNormalizer`/`AdaptiveBinarizer` ever see them.
1. **`PaperRegionMasker`** ([`Sources/ScanTailorCore/Binarization/PaperRegionMasker.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorCore/Binarization/PaperRegionMasker.swift)):
   - Classifies paper vs. desk the same way `DocumentQuadDetector` does (`localMean − localStd`, Otsu, second split for a light desk with dark text), then **flood-fills only the non-paper pixels connected to the image border** — interior dark content (ink, illustrations) that never reaches the edge is never touched.
   - **Trust gate before anything is masked**: local variance right at the candidate boundary must exceed the paper's own baseline variance a bit further out by `minimumBoundarySharpnessStd` (default `3.0`). A physical paper edge is a step, so this passes a real desk however low-contrast (laminate); a smooth illumination gradient or vignette has no discontinuity, so it fails and is left alone — this is what keeps the masker from eating shadowed corners on lit, deskless pages (the regression that surfaced against Substep 4.2.1/4.2.2's shaded fixtures during implementation).
   - **Mode**: `full` when the border-connected desk covers more than `residualModeAreaThreshold` (`0.25`) of the frame (no rectification, or a rejected/disabled detection) — masks the whole extent found, unless the "paper" class's own convex hull also spans nearly the whole frame with no clean border ring (bare wood grain with no document at all, mirroring `DocumentQuadDetector`'s frame-filling check), in which case nothing is masked. `residual` otherwise (the normal post-rectification case) — masking is restricted to within `residualDepthFraction` (`0.08`) of each edge and capped at `residualAreaCap` (`0.15`) of total area; exceeding the cap skips masking entirely and flags the page, so a misclassification can only leave visible desk for 5.2.2 to clean up, never erase content.
   - **Full-resolution boundary refinement**: the coarse mask is eroded by one working pixel for safety, then regrown only through full-resolution pixels darker than the paper/desk brightness midpoint within a thin band — this is what lets text within a couple of pixels of the true paper edge survive.
   - **Fill**: masked pixels are replaced with a paper-toned estimate (per-block paper means, inpainted across the masked area with `BackgroundNormalizer.inpaintAndSmoothNormalized2D`, bilinearly upsampled) rather than pure white or exclusion from local statistics — matching tone avoids creating a false-ink seam next to shadowed paper, and needed no API changes to the binarizer, normalizer, or either `ComputeAccelerator` warp backend.
   - `DeskMaskResult` (`Codable`): `mode` (`residual`/`full`/`skipped`), `maskedFraction`, `maskedDepthBySide`, `paperBrightness`, `deskBrightness`, `flags` (`skipped:<reason>`, `capped:depth`, `capped:coverage`).
2. **Pipeline** ([`BookPipelineCoordinator.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorPipeline/BookPipelineCoordinator.swift), [`SourcePageGeometry.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorPipeline/SourcePageGeometry.swift)): `maskDeskOfSourcePage(_:record:)` runs immediately after `rectifySourcePage(...)` in the Pass-1 loop, on every source page (rectified or not). New `Configuration` fields: `enableDeskMasking` (default `true`), `deskMaskerConfiguration`. Flatbed (`fullFrame`) pages and anything the trust gate rejects pass through byte-identical.
3. **Manifest & overrides**: `SourcePageGeometryRecord.deskMask: DeskMaskResult?` (`decodeIfPresent`, so older manifests load with `nil`). `SourcePageGeometryOverride.disableDeskMask: Bool?` — when set, the record's `deskMask` stays `nil` rather than recording a skip, carried forward through the existing `--manifest` re-run path.
4. **CLI**: `--no-desk-mask`; the manifest summary prints `Desk Masking: N masked (mean X %) | N skipped | N capped`.
5. **Tests**: [`Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/DeskMaskingTests.swift`](../Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/DeskMaskingTests.swift) (7 tests): mask-vs-truth IoU ≥ 0.98 on a wood desk, border-ink density before/after raw binarization, bent-spread gutter wedges masked inside the rectified frame, ≥ 99 % ink survival within a few pixels of the true paper edge, flatbed passthrough (mask and pipeline output both byte-identical), low-contrast laminate still masked alongside safe wood-only/plain-grey/degenerate-size skips, and pipeline integration (manifest telemetry, `disableDeskMask` override, legacy decoding, CLI flag, summary line). `126/126` pass across 21 suites, 0 warnings on a clean build.
6. **Deviation from the approved design**: the written design proposed sharing the paper/desk threshold with `DocumentQuadDetector` directly; the shipped version reclassifies independently (simpler, no changes to the already-tested detector) but needed the added boundary-sharpness trust gate above — found only once full-pipeline tests (Substeps 4.2.1/4.2.2) were run, since those fixtures' deliberate illumination ramps on deskless pages are exactly what a brightness-only classifier confuses with a desk edge.

### 3.9 Recently Completed: Substep 5.2.2 (Macro-Cluster Border Cleaning Fallback)
Closes Phase 5. Desk that survives rectification and masking in a way the standard border rules cannot see is now excised, but only on pages whose own geometry telemetry says confidence was marginal.
1. **The gap**: `BorderAndSpeckleCleaner.removeScanBorders` erases multi-edge components and edge bars up to 2× the 14 % strip limit, but (G1) desk *detached* from the frame edge by a white band (the `capped:depth` paint band, deskew's white corner fill) is never near an edge, and (G2) single-edge blocks deeper than that limit or shorter than 20 % of the side are kept.
2. **Algorithm** ([`MacroBorderFallback.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorCore/Binarization/MacroBorderFallback.swift), `BorderAndSpeckleCleaner.removeMacroBorderBlocks`): coarse grid (cell = short side / 64); a cell is dark at black density ≥ `0.30`. Per eligible side and per grid line, scan inward: leading cells must be empty (≤ 5 %) for at most 10 % of the page (bridges the paint band), then a solid run of dark cells (solidity ≥ 0.85, at most one missed cell). Contiguous lines form a candidate, accepted only if deeper than the standard strip limit, ≤ 35 % deep (`tooDeep`), ≥ 15 % of the edge (`shortSpan`), solid (`notSolid`), and **not text-like**: the fraction of blank pixel rows in the candidate must be ≤ 0.15 (`textLike`; text has line gaps, binarized grain or solid blocks have none). Excision erases black pixels in the candidate rectangle, then components lying wholly inside a 1-cell ring around it.
   - **Design finding**: the 0.30 threshold exists because binarized wood grain is a *noisy ~40 % black*, not solid. Density alone, and micro-block emptiness, cannot tell that from dense text (the binarized noise is spatially correlated); the blank-row test on whole candidates is what does. A flat synthetic block even binarizes to an outline only, so fixtures must be textured.
3. **Gating** (`SourcePageGeometryRecord.borderFallbackContext`): engages only on triggers `quad:rejected` (unless a full-mode mask handled it), `quad:marginal` (confidence < `borderFallbackMarginalConfidence`, default `0.70`), `quad:occluded:*`, `quad:cornerFallback:*`, `quad:lowContrastDesk`, `mask:capped:depth|coverage`, `mask:skipped:noSharpBoundary|noPaper`, or a `forced` override. Confident pages and flatbed scans pass through byte-identical. The gutter side of a `leftVerso`/`rightRecto` slice is never eligible.
4. **API**: `clean(_:fallback:) -> (bitmap, MacroBorderResult?)`; `clean(_:)` is unchanged. `BorderAndSpeckleCleaner.Configuration.macroBorder` (`MacroBorderConfiguration`, clamped; config decoder uses `decodeIfPresent`).
5. **Manifest/CLI**: `PageProcessingRecord.borderFallback: MacroBorderResult?` (`triggers`, `excisedFraction`, `excisedDepthBySide`, `rejectedCandidates`; `nil` = not engaged). `PageManualOverride.borderFallback: BorderFallbackPolicy?` (`automatic`/`forced`/`disabled`). Coordinator `Configuration.enableBorderFallback` / `borderFallbackMarginalConfidence`; CLI `--no-border-fallback`; summary line `Border Fallback: N excised | N engaged, nothing found | N rejected`. For the post-dewarp cleaning the dewarped result is recorded.
6. **Tests**: [`MacroBorderFallbackTests.swift`](../Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/MacroBorderFallbackTests.swift) (7): deep block and detached remnant excised with text untouched, wood-grain block excised, legitimate content survives (text near the edge, a dense narrow text column, short block, 45 %-deep figure, gutter side), passthrough and legacy config decoding, context builder triggers/sides/overrides, and pipeline integration (forced/disabled/flag, textured-desk binarization, JSON round trip, legacy manifest, CLI flag). `133/133` pass across 22 suites, 0 warnings.
7. **Known limitations**: a dense, uniform halftone or hatched figure (no blank rows) that touches an outer edge, is 14–35 % deep and ≥ 15 % of the edge, on a *marginal* page, is indistinguishable from grain and would be excised; use `borderFallback: disabled` for that page. The font-scale probe pass still sees unrectified, unmasked pages.
8. **Pitfall**: when adding a CLI flag in `parseProcessOptions`, every `case` must advance `i`; omitting it makes the parser spin forever (found here, hung the whole test run).

### 3.10 Recently Completed: Substep 6.1.1 (Running Header & Top Baseline Detection)
Opens Phase 6. Every page now gets a **top anchor**: the running header's baseline when the page carries a recurring header, otherwise the first body baseline (release criterion 4). **Telemetry only — no pixels change**; 6.1.2 consumes the anchors.
1. **Why**: `ContentBoxDetector` already keeps header/folio clusters inside the content box but never labels them, and Tier 1 pages are aligned by content-box top. A page without a header, or with a header at a different distance, therefore jumps.
2. **Per-page profile** ([`RunningHeaderDetector.swift`](../Sources/ScanTailorCore/Layout/RunningHeaderDetector.swift), `profilePage`): a word-popcount row ink profile over the content box (override box if set) splits text lines. Bands > 1.7× typical height are split at a near-empty row (≤ 20 % of peak) to separate touching lines; thin, wide, solid bands are **rules** folded into the line above (`hasRule`); accent-sized bands join the nearest line. Each line records bounds, **baseline** (first row below the ≥ 50 %-of-peak core), core height, and ink. Body pitch / core height are medians over lines *below* the top line. The top and bottom lines are split into ink segments at gaps ≥ 2.5 core heights for folio detection.
3. **Book clustering** (`analyzeBook`): per logical side (`verso`, `recto`, `single`). A top line is a **candidate** when the next baseline is ≥ 1.5 body pitches below it and its core height is 0.35–1.75× body core (taller = `titleLike`). Candidates are clustered mode-seeking on *(offset in pitches, height ratio)* with tolerances 0.35 pitch / 0.20; a cluster confirms running headers with ≥ 3 pages and ≥ 30 % of the side's text pages. Absolute page position is deliberately *not* used (camera captures shift it). Sides with < 3 text pages accept candidates at low confidence (`book:tooFewPages`).
4. **Folios**: in the header line, the outer-end segment (verso left, recto right, single either) no wider than 4.5 header core heights; a header line that is itself that narrow is a folio-only header. A footer folio is a single narrow segment ≥ 1.5 pitches below the last body line; it is never taken as body.
5. **Anchor** (`PageTopAnchor`): `kind` (`runningHeader` / `bodyBaseline` / `none`), `anchorY`, `headerBox`, `headerBaselineY`, `folioBox`, `footerFolioBox`, `firstBodyLineTopY`, `firstBodyBaselineY`, `confidence`, `flags` (`header:outOfCluster`, `header:sizeMismatch`, `titleLike`, `sinkage`, `book:tooFewPages`, `override:*`, `noText`). Coordinates are pass-1 page coordinates at processing DPI (after split/deskew/dewarp, before framing) — 6.1.2 must map them through the layout plan.
6. **Manifest/overrides/CLI**: `PageProcessingRecord.topAnchor`, `BookProcessingManifest.runningHeaderModel` (`RunningHeaderModel.sides`: page/header/footer-folio counts, body pitch and core, cluster center), both `decodeIfPresent`. `PageManualOverride.runningHeader: RunningHeaderPolicy?` (`automatic` / `present` = top line is the header / `absent` = top line is body). Coordinator `Configuration.enableRunningHeaderDetection` (default `true`) + `runningHeaderConfiguration`; no CLI flag (output is unchanged). Summary line `Running Headers: verso H/N · recto H/N | N body-anchored | N none`. Runs between pass 1 and pass 2; geometry only, no OCR.
7. **Tests**: [`RunningHeaderDetectionTests.swift`](../Tests/ScanTailorCoreTests/RunningHeaderDetectionTests.swift) (7): line profile (baseline/core/pitch ±2 px, blank regions), header rule and touching-line split, 10-page alternating book with ±40 px jitter (headers + outer folios confirmed), chapter opener (`titleLike` + `sinkage`), headerless page and footer folio, scattered section headings forming no cluster, `present`/`absent` overrides + `tooFewPages` + blank page + config clamping, and pipeline integration (anchors recorded, override, **identical output bitmaps with detection off**, JSON round trip, legacy manifest). `140/140` pass across 23 suites, 0 warnings.
8. **Known limitations**: headers the content-box detector already rejected (gap > 14 % of page height) are out of reach; two-column bodies starting at different heights give a noisy first baseline; a body whose first line is an unusual heading is measured off and simply falls out of the cluster (flagged). Synthetic fixtures only — validate on the real corpus during 6.1.2.
